## Requests

1. Record receipt date, requested right and minimum contact data in a private, access-controlled register. Acknowledge receipt and identify the processing concerned. Do not request passwords, cookies, tokens or course content.
2. Check identity proportionately only when needed. Distinguish locally controlled app data, information held for support, telemetry, and university/Webex data. Route third-party service requests appropriately; do not promise authority over their records.
3. Respond without undue delay and within one month under GDPR Article 12(3). A justified extension of up to two further months must be explained within the original month. Document refusals, applicable exceptions and complaint routes.
4. For local erasure, guide the user; do not access their device without separate authorization. For telemetry establish whether records can actually be linked and the provider's assistance; GDPR Article 11 does not require collecting extra identifiers solely for identification. Opt-out only stops future sends.
5. Send exports privately, verify recipient and exclude other people's data. Retain only evidence necessary to handle/account for the request; delete support/privacy correspondence within 90 days after closure, except documented legal obligations or disputes.

## Local cleanup guidance

Disconnect the university account and disable Recordings. Resolve any deletion error in Settings → Data and privacy before assuming session deletion succeeded. Revoke the university mobile token through the university's supported mechanism if revocation is needed; deleting a local token does not revoke it remotely.

For a single account, use Settings → Recordings → “Forget this account’s study data”. This removes its watchlist, viewing state and resume points, closes playback, and preserves downloaded materials. A persistent warning and retry report physical history deletion failures. This is separate from signing out.

For a whole-app reset, quit BeepBar. Preserve the materials folder and local edits first. App state is in `~/Library/Application Support/Beepbar` and preferences for bundle `io.github.tvaccari.beepbar`; removing all of these resets app state, including sync history and per-account study choices. This is a whole-app reset, not a per-account erasure operation. Check the selected materials folder separately; do not delete it unintentionally. Uninstalling the app alone is not data erasure. Backups, macOS notifications, clipboard/Now Playing and system-managed diagnostics need separate consideration; do not promise remote or backup erasure from an app reset. Relaunch only after intentionally completing the reset because enabled features can create new data.

## Suspected breach

Record discovery time, affected data/systems, access, scope, likely consequences and mitigation privately. Contain the issue, restrict access/revoke exposed tokens where authorized, preserve minimal evidence, and contact the privacy owner immediately. Never attach secrets or unredacted user logs to public issues.

Assess GDPR Articles 33–34: notify the competent authority within 72 hours of awareness where required; document delay and report in stages if necessary. Inform affected people without undue delay where high risk requires it, subject to the legal exceptions. Document the assessment even if no notification is required. Do not automatically classify every bug as a reportable breach.

## DPIA and DPO screening — 2026-10-19

**DPO:** no mandatory appointment identified for the described configuration. The publisher is not a public authority; its activity does not perform large-scale individual monitoring, profiling or large-scale special-category/criminal-data processing. Daily v2 counts do not carry an individual key. The privacy contact is the responsible owner, not a DPO with conflicting management duties. Reassess on individual analytics, sensitive-data purposes or material scale changes. Source: [EDPB DPO guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/data-protection-officer_en), checked 2026-10-19.

**DPIA screening:** assess each operation above, not only app size. No scoring, legal/significant automated decision, dataset matching, systematic individual tracking or service exclusion based on analytics; no biometric/location/special-category collection by the publisher is designed. Students may be minors (vulnerability criterion); credentials and private study state are confidentiality risks. Native local synchronization/playback is established technology, not an innovative high-risk use merely because this app is new. State stays on the user's Mac, with bounded session retention, deletion controls and no publisher monitoring of learning. The combination as described does not indicate a likely high risk requiring a full DPIA; safeguards do not substitute for a DPIA where required; this is a documented screening, not a claim that local data can never be sensitive. Reopen before centralizing learning history, adding individual linkage or scoring, targeting children, sensitive purposes, or discovering inadequate safeguards. Check actual scope/scale again before release; no current user total is inferred from an old lower bound. Sources: [Garante DPIA list and criteria](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/9058979), [EDPB accountability and records](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en), checked 2026-10-19.
